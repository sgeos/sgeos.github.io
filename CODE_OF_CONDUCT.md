# Code of Conduct

This project asks very little of the people who read it, and it asks little of the
people who take part in it. Its prose is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 and its
code is licensed under 0BSD, so anyone may take either and do anything with it. This
code governs only how people treat each other in the project's own spaces, which are its
repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, the comments on its posts, and any
channel the project runs.

## Expected

- Stay on topic. The project's spaces exist to improve the blog and discuss its posts,
  and discussion that does not serve that belongs elsewhere.
- Treat others as capable people acting in good faith, and argue with their ideas,
  not with them.
- Disagree openly and with evidence. Corrections are welcome, and so is being shown to
  be wrong.

## Judged on merit

Participation is judged on its merits alone. A correction, a report or an argument is
weighed by whether it is correct and whether it improves the blog, without regard to the
contributor's politics, beliefs, identity or affiliations, or to anything else they say
or do outside the project.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, threats, or insults directed at a person.
- Unwelcome sexual attention, and demeaning remarks about personal characteristics.
- Publishing another person's private information without their permission.
- Deliberately disrupting the project's spaces, including repeated off-topic material.

## Automated contributors

Agents and bots that take part in the project's spaces are held to the same standard.
The person or organisation that operates an agent is responsible for what it posts.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove any contribution, and may restrict or block anyone
who does not follow this code, in proportion to what happened. To report a concern,
contact the maintainer privately. Reports are kept confidential as far as the law allows.
Nothing here creates a contract or a right to be heard. It records how the maintainer
intends to act.
