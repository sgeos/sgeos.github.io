# Analytical Essay Structure

> **Navigation**: [Writing](./README.md) | [Documentation Root](../README.md)

Section structure for the analytical essay and survey genre. The pattern was established by the BTRON article and held across the fixed-wing unmanned-aerial-vehicle series and the patents-and-startup series at A161 through A172. See [Article Genres](./ARTICLE_GENRES.md) for genre identification.

## Section Order

Analytical essays and surveys do not use the keystone structure. They follow this pattern.

1. Opening prose with no heading. Frame the topic, state what the article will and will not do, and cross-link any companion articles in the series.
2. A brief history section where the subject has one. History motivates the analysis that follows.
3. Custom analytical sections named for their content rather than from a fixed template. Headings like Survey, Mechanism, Where the Model Wins, Where It Is the Wrong Fit, Comparison, and Strategy are typical. Choose headings for the argument.
4. `## Epistemic State` section. See the [Epistemic State Convention](#the-epistemic-state-convention) below. This is the genre signature and is expected in any essay that mixes fact and judgment.
5. `## Out of Scope` section enumerating deferred topics, so completeness is never implied where it is absent.
6. `## Conclusion` section summarising the argument.
7. `## References` section with the sorted, categorized reference index.

## The Epistemic State Convention

An essay that makes claims of mixed certainty should include an Epistemic State section that sorts its claims into classes and tells the reader which is which. The BTRON article uses three classes: historical fact, structural analysis, and market inference. The section names which specific claims were softened or corrected during review, and it invites disagreement on the appropriate grounds.

This convention aligns with the global rigor standard and is the essay genre's equivalent of the worked-example verification that anchors a subsystem deep-dive.

A typical Epistemic State section enumerates:

- The factual claims the article makes and the sources behind them.
- The analytical claims the article makes and the reasoning behind them.
- Any claims that were softened, qualified, or corrected during review.
- The bounds within which the reader should treat the argument as authoritative.

## Length

Length follows the subject rather than a target. Essays in this genre have run from roughly four hundred lines for a focused piece to several thousand for a comprehensive survey such as BTRON. Do not pad an essay to the subsystem deep-dive length.

## Mathematics

Analytical essays carry equations only where a real quantitative relationship exists, for example a funnel expressed as a product of conditional probabilities or an expected-value model. Some essays correctly carry none. Do not add token equations to satisfy a density target that belongs to a different genre. See [MathJax Conventions](./MATHJAX_CONVENTIONS.md).
