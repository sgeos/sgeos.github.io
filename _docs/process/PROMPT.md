#### Task Binding and Verification

Deterministic smart contracts are assigned to R miners for resolution.
Miners also receive salt.
The smart contract return value is hashed using a standard function.
The hash and salt are used for a trivial proof of work exercise.
The miner is responsible for returning the smart contract result,
and the answer to its proof of work exercise.

#### The Economic Loop

The time-value cost imposed on holders guarantees a baseline subsidy.
Fees may optionally be paid.

#### Dispute Resolution

The protocol periodically issues verification license tasks to random miners.
Miners who pass these tasks are elevated to licensed verifier status for the
duration of their license.
Miners who fail these tasks forfeit their participation stake and are
booted from the mining pool.
Booted miners must stake another 1.0 coin to be readmitted to the pool.
Honest mining operators will reevalute the reliability of booted miners.
Consider adding a new section for this information.
