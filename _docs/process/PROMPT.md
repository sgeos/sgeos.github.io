### A104 Topic

The topic for A104 is: "Steampunk and Analog Electronics for Von Neumann Probe Control."

Structure the article as follows.

INTRODUCTION

- Begin with an introduction that forward-summarizes the article.
- Define the following terms:
  - "Steampunk Electronics"
  - "Analog Electronics"
  - "Analog Steampunk Electronics"
- Explain what these technologies are, what their typical applications are,
  and why they may be promising for Von Neumann probe control systems.

MAIN SECTIONS

Write three main sections covering:

1. Steampunk Electronics
2. Analog Electronics
3. Analog Steampunk Electronics (hybrid or overlapping approaches)

For each of these sections, address the following topics in order:

- Historical origins of the technology.
- Key historical and modern examples of implementations or partial solutions.
- The current state of the art in the field.
- Contemporary real-world applications.
- The performance requirements for Von Neumann probes.
- How the current state of the art compares to those requirements.
- Work in progress, partial solutions, and promising research directions.
- Hypothetical or extrapolated approaches that might meet probe requirements.

CONCLUSION

- Provide a conclusion that backward-summarizes the article.

SUPPLEMENTARY MATERIAL
