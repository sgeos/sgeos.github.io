### A106 Topic

"Two-Stage Flying Delta Wing Vehicles for Civil and National Security Applications"

The concept explored in this article is a **two-stage aerospace vehicle composed entirely of flying delta wings**.

In this architecture:

- The **combined vehicle** is a pure flying delta wing.
- The **second stage** is a pure flying delta wing that retains the **nose and forward geometry** of the combined vehicle.
- The **first stage** is the **bottom stage** and retains the **rear geometry** of the combined vehicle after separation.

After staging:

- The **second stage separates upward and forward**, retaining the nose and forward aerodynamic surfaces.
- The **first stage continues flight as a truncated or noseless flying delta wing derived from the rear geometry of the combined vehicle**.

Both stages are capable of **independent atmospheric flight** after separation.

Operational goals:

- The **first stage** accelerates the vehicle through the dense lower atmosphere, separates, and **returns to base**.
- The **second stage** continues the mission, which may include:
  - orbital insertion,
  - hypersonic transport,
  - or atmospheric reentry and mission execution.

The **combined vehicle and second stage remain pure flying delta wings**, while the **first stage becomes a truncated delta wing after separation**.
