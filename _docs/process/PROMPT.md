### Strengthen Scientific Defensibility

#### Soften Absolutist Claims Without Weakening Direction

Replace categorical phrasing with conditional rigor:

- “Explains the silence” → “Is sufficient to explain the silence.”
- “There is no second place” → “Under competitive expansion assumptions, second place becomes strategically unstable.”
- “Detection implies defeat” → “Under worst-case competitive assumptions, detection implies extreme strategic risk.”

This preserves the deterministic trajectory while shielding the thesis from trivial dismissal.

---

#### Reduce False Precision

Avoid highly specific probabilistic or numerical claims unless derived explicitly:

- Round model outputs (e.g., 0.7276 → ~0.73).
- Replace precise fractions of universe control with “on the order of half under central parameters.”
- Replace exact multiplicative factors (e.g., 100× radius) with “orders of magnitude increase in surface area.”

Precision signals rigor only when derivations are visible.

---

#### Add Engineering Caveats Where Energy Is Invoked

Particularly in:

- AGN-driven sterilization

- CMB-temperature masking
- Artificial red dwarf construction

Add short constraint clauses:

- “In principle…”
- “Subject to extreme engineering challenges…”
- “Assuming sufficiently advanced beam control…”

One clause per section is enough to dramatically increase credibility.
