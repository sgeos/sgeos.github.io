written into the same file. (1) Nomenclature and symbol standards of engineering disciplines are kept under
(e) as nomenclature systems, while nomenclature of natural or chemical objects is dropped. (2) Obituaries,
session headers and meeting programmes are non-works, and a demonstrator or unmanned-aircraft record that
reports only a discipline result (aerodynamics, heating, aeroelasticity, handling qualities, flight control,
air-data calibration, inlet testing, computational aerodynamics) is dropped. Both were re-applied to every
title already read and to the blind sample. Refinement 1 flips one sample title, row 191, "Nomenclature and

finds 7 of 300 (2.33 percent, Wilson 1.13 to 4.74 percent) and agrees with the full reading title by title.
