````
### The Survey Was Probed With the Article's Conclusions

**A survey assembled around an article's topics can be silent on the article's results.** Nine conclusions were measured against the survey, first in the article's own words and then in the words a publisher would use in a title.

| Conclusion the article states | Article's words | Field's words | After harvest |
|---|---|---|---|
| hover performance against prediction | 47 | 385 | 402 |
| the advancing blade as the binding limit | 12 | 64 | 207 |
| a retrofit rather than a clean sheet | 4 | 57 | 173 |
| vibration as the deliverable | 62 | 204 | 227 |
| slowing the rotor to go faster | 36 | 129 | 173 |
| an old technology demonstrated late | 2 | 22 | 120 |
| the transmission as the constraint | 64 | 136 | 152 |
| the duct doing three jobs at once | 44 | 132 | 132 |
| the designation and how aircraft are numbered | 1 | 6 | 7 |

**Seven of the nine measured thin in the article's own words and all seven opened on being restated.** The keystone is the clearest case. **The advancing blade as the binding limit stood at 12 records** when asked for as this article phrases it, and at 64 once the question admitted compressibility, drag divergence, transonic flow and blade tips, which is what the literature calls it. **A harvest then took it to 207.**

**Two survived broadening and only one of them was harvested.**

**An old technology demonstrated late stood at 22 and went to 120**, being a real subject with a real literature in programme management and technology maturation.

**The designation subject stood at 6 and was deliberately left there.** The X-44 article already ran that experiment for this series. Eight queries for designation systems, nomenclature, records management and classification returned 1,510 records **including Massachusetts tax valuations of 1771, salmonella serotype naming and dental implant designation systems**. Thirteen survived an aerospace gate and every one was component nomenclature. That cluster was built, read and removed, on the finding that **a cluster of thirteen off-subject records is worse than no cluster because it looks like coverage.**

**The series has already paid for that measurement and the answer does not need buying twice.** How aircraft are numbered is a subject whose vocabulary does not discriminate, and the claim about the skipped designation therefore stands on the sponsor's own account rather than on a survey. **That is the same position the X-46 article and the X-47 article reached about arguments of their own that fell outside their gates**, and it is the honest one.
````

````
### How Aircraft Are Numbered Has No Discriminating Vocabulary

**The designation subject has no literature that a title search can separate from its homonyms.** The words designation, nomenclature and classification also name Massachusetts tax valuations of 1771, salmonella serotype naming and dental implant designation systems, and in the aerospace literature they name components rather than aircraft. **How aircraft are numbered is a subject whose vocabulary does not discriminate**, and the claim about the skipped designation therefore stands on the sponsor's own account rather than on a survey.
````

````
### What the Survey Admits, Excludes and Keeps

**The survey admits a record only when a person reading its title finds it on this article's subject.** Many of the words this subject depends on also name something else, and a record that shares only such a word is excluded. Rotor blade also names the compressor and turbine blades of turbomachinery, and rotor also names electric motors and generators, the shafts, bearings and pumps of industrial machinery, and rotor spun yarn. Drag reduction also names polymer, surfactant, microbubble and air-layer drag reduction in liquids, much of it on ship hulls, and drag also names embedment anchors and cross-country skiing. Weight estimation also names the weighing of cattle, patients, fruit and gemstones and the estimation of foetal weight. Ducted also names radio, acoustic and heat transfer ducts, the ducted rocket and ramjet of solid-propellant missile propulsion, and domestic air conditioning. Atmosphere also names conditioning atmospheres, autorotation has senses that are not a rotor's, auxiliary propulsion and thrusters have spacecraft senses, vehicle also names road and rail vehicles, and wing also names a military unit. **Editorial notices are excluded as well, because a survey counts research works.** A record goes when its digital object identifier carries the numbered notice suffix the American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics gives a correction, or when its title opens with Correction, Correction to, Errata or Retracted Article, and the index from the back matter of a book is excluded for the same reason.

**Some records of doubtful relevance are kept**, being marine ducted propellers, pump-jets and propulsors, tidal, wind and hydrokinetic turbines, aircraft-engine rotor dynamics, rocket nozzle and missile thrust vectoring, launch vehicle, reentry and balloon gondola tests, the hydrodynamic drag of submerged bodies and towed arrays, robots and submersibles driven by ducted fans, and the high-lift wings of racing cars. Each shares the physics of a duct, a rotor, vectored thrust or drag, or the practice of flight test, with this subject, so each is kept and a reader may weigh it accordingly.

**Every research title has been read for relevance, so the off-topic share that remains is a matter of reading judgement and not of sampling, and no sample can put a figure on it.**
````
