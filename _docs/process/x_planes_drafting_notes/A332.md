### Consistency pass follow-up, the unstick chain rebuilt on the primary record (9 October)

The basis was wrong. The article derived C_L = 1.036 from an 80 kt unstick with the rear nozzle aft at
34,000 lb, and the three later results all used it. Tomassetti (ref_tomassetti) records the Mission X
unstick at 80 kt "after only 200 feet", with the thrust vectored to 60 degrees. ref_lm_conversion records
the 9 July conversion at 180 kt after a climb to 9,000 ft.

What was rebuilt, and how each value moved:
- **Mission X unstick.** The inequality L + T_f + T_r sin 60 >= W gives T_v = 39,488 lbf. Taking 18,000
  lbf for the rear nozzle at 80 kt is a labelled assumption. The required C_L is zero or negative below
  39,488 lb and at most 0.206 at the 41,500 lb ceiling. **The record cannot pin a coefficient.** The value
  1.036 is withdrawn.
- **The 26 July deflection.** The single 14.21 degrees became a table over weight, 34,000 and 41,500 lb,
  and C_L of 0, 0.5 and 1.0. It runs from 14.86 to 34.13 degrees at 34,000 lb and from 42.31 to 77.90
  degrees at 41,500 lb. Every entry is inside the 95 degree range. "The two takeoffs differ by about
  fourteen degrees of nozzle" is withdrawn, since the record gives 60 degrees for Mission X.
- **The corridor.** The 146.8 kt figure is withdrawn because it rested on 1.036. The claim now rests on the
  record: the aircraft was flying conventionally at 180 kt when the fan engaged. At sea-level density, the
  same assumption the momentum drag uses, the wing was at a C_L between 0.537 and 0.841 for weights from
  26,500 to 41,500 lb. The Contemporary Literature sentence that cited 146.8 was narrowed to match.
- **The ground roll.** The model now has the nozzle aft until unstick, as the Mission X account describes.
  This is an assumption for 26 July. The minimum roll went from 314.9 ft to 307.4 to 380.0 ft. The quoted
  roll over the minimum went from 1.588 to between 1.316 and 1.627. Available acceleration went from 0.506
  g to between 0.518 and 0.419 g. The share used went from 0.630 to between 0.615 and 0.760. **The finding
  that 500 ft exceeds the minimum survives at every weight.**
- **The 200 ft check.** Reaching 80 kt from rest in 200 ft needs 1.417 g. With all 41,900 lbf pointed
  aft, no drag and no friction, the floor weight allows at most 1.397 g. On the rear nozzle alone the
  frictionless minimum is 472 ft. **The recorded 200 ft and 80 kt cannot both describe a roll from brake
  release, so they disagree.** The article relies on the speed.
