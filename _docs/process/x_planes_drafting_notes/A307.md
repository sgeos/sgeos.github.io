[Stellar-inertial navigation, star trackers and celestial reference] **The external observation that resets a growing error.** Star trackers and star sensors, star identification and centroiding, stellar-inertial and astro-inertial integration, and the celestial navigation practice the automatic tracker mechanised. 87 titles name a satellite, a spacecraft or an orbit, which is where the automatic tracker went after the cruise missile. **21 titles name daytime or daylight star tracking**, the demanding case the Navaho had to solve inside the atmosphere.

[Landing gear, brakes, arresting gear and decelerators] **Where the fleet was lost.** Landing gear dynamics and loads, brakes and tyres, arresting gear and barricades, and drag chutes and decelerators. **97 titles name shimmy**, the directional instability of a gear on the runway.
