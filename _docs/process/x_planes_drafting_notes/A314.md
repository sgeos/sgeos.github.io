> **The partition seen from the ground.** Shock tubes, shock and expansion tunnels, arc jets and plasma wind tunnels, ballistic ranges and hypersonic wind tunnels, with their calibration and diagnostics. **Expansion tubes and tunnels, which reach flight enthalpy for a fraction of a millisecond, are named in 117 titles and arc-heated facilities, which run for minutes on a contaminated stream, in 77**, so the facility trade the article describes is visible in the counts.

> ### High-temperature air, nonequilibrium chemistry and rarefied flow
>
> **The chemistry the X-17 surrendered.** Dissociation and ionisation, vibrational and chemical nonequilibrium, state-to-state and multi-temperature models, catalytic walls, rarefied flow and the direct simulation Monte Carlo method. **The communication blackout, which the X-17's enthalpy never produced, has a literature of its own**, with 55 plasma-sheath, antenna and signal titles running from 1959 to 2026.

> ### The X-17, ballistic missiles and re-entry vehicles
>
> **The vehicle, its customers and its contemporaries.** The X-17 itself, Operation Argus, the Atlas, Titan, Thor and Polaris programmes, the Trailblazer, Project Fire and Reentry F flight programmes, and the ballistic missile and its re-entry vehicle in design, flight test, dynamics and tracking. **One title in the survey names the X-17**, and it is the 1965 contractor report on flights R-2 and R-9 on which this article's flight-data sections rest, so the vehicle's literature is almost entirely the literature of the programmes it fed.

> ### Stagnation-point and blunt-body heating
>
> **The measurement the vehicle was built to make.** Stagnation-point and blunt-body convective heating, its correlations and their validation, and the engineering methods that predict heating along a trajectory. **Spikes, aerodisks and counterflowing jets that reduce drag and heating on a blunt nose are the recent growth**, with 15 of its 25 such titles dating from 2015 or later.

> ### Planetary entry, descent and landing
>
> **Where the X-17's question went.** Entry, descent and landing at Mars, Venus, Titan and the giant planets, aerocapture, inflatable decelerators, parachutes and supersonic retropropulsion, and the reconstruction of flown entries. **Mars is named in 221 of its 441 titles**, about half, and no other body in more than 18.

> ### Ablation and the response of ablative heat shields
>
> **The answer to the heat that arrives anyway.** Charring and subliming ablators, pyrolysis, recession and shape change, arc-jet and flight validation of material response codes, and the re-entry ablation of nuclear fuel forms, which had to be predicted because they were meant to burn up or to survive by design.

> ### Thermal protection materials, ceramics and hot structures
>
> **What thermal protection became besides ablation.** Reusable tiles and insulation, metallic and hot structures, transpiration and active cooling, and ultra-high-temperature ceramics. **Those ceramics are the recent centre of the cluster**, with 46 of its 73 ceramic titles dating from 2015 or later, and hafnium compounds, the family of the single survivor in this article's re-radiation table, appear in nine.

> ### Shock-layer radiation
>
> **The mechanism velocity alone put out of the X-17's reach.** Equilibrium and nonequilibrium shock-layer radiation, collisional-radiative models, radiative transfer and its coupling to ablation, and the shock-tube and flight measurements that test them. **Flown capsules anchor the cluster in flight**, Stardust, Hayabusa, Fire, Apollo and OSIRIS-REx among them, in 34 titles.

> ### Re-entry trajectory, ballistic entry and guidance
>
> **The curve of which the X-17 made one point.** Ballistic and lifting entry trajectories, the Allen-Eggers solution and its extensions, guidance, trajectory optimisation and the estimation of a re-entering body's state. **Glide vehicles fill the modern part**, named in 104 of the 154 titles from 2015 onward.

> ### Flight experiments and free-flight measurement
>
> **Flight as the ground truth.** Re-entry flight experiments from the sounding-rocket and Shuttle eras to SHEFEX, EXPERT, HIFiRE and the sample return capsules, with their instrumentation and reconstruction. **Infrasound, seismic and acoustic observation of returning capsules supplies 18 titles**, which is free flight measured from the ground.

> ### Spacecraft demise, debris re-entry and casualty risk
>
> **The relations run in reverse.** Design for demise, breakup and survivability analysis, casualty risk, the deliberate re-entry of nuclear power sources and the atmospheric effects of demise. **Meteors are the natural case of the same physics**, and 51 of its titles treat the entry of meteors, meteoroids and cosmic dust.

> ### Re-entry body aerodynamics, dynamics and control
>
> **The body as a vehicle.** The aerodynamics, stability, trim and dynamics of capsules and lifting bodies, and the attitude and flight control of re-entry and hypersonic vehicles.

> ### Uncertainty quantification and model validation
>
> **Knowing what is wrong, as a method.** Uncertainty quantification, sensitivity analysis, Bayesian calibration, surrogate models and code verification applied to re-entry flows, heating, radiation and material response. **The discipline is recent but not new**, with nine of its titles predating 2000.

> ### Computational methods and the hypersonic test cases
>
> **The numerical substrate.** Euler, Navier-Stokes and particle methods for hypersonic flow and the standard validation cases, the double ellipse, the compression ramp and the delta wing, that the computational community built to test them.

> ### Solid rocket motors and staging
>
> **The vehicle's own kind of propulsion.** Solid-propellant motors, their grains, cases, ignition and combustion stability, and multistage staging and optimisation.

> ### Hypersonic cruise vehicles, design and thermal management
>
> **The X-17's problem re-encountered.** Air-breathing and combined-cycle hypersonic vehicles, their design, and the thermal management of vehicles that must sustain the heating for minutes rather than survive it for seconds.

> ### Other re-entry and hypersonic literature
>
> **The residual, reported and not hidden.** General treatments of hypersonic flow and flight, programme overviews, re-entry module and capsule concepts, recovery techniques and single-subject papers that belong to no cluster above. **It is small because the clusters above are specific**, and a reader may treat it as the survey's margin.
