### The Definition of Primary

**Primacy is a property of the document, and this survey derives it from the identifier.** A record is a report primary when it is a NASA Technical Reports Server url or carries a digital object identifier under the 10.21236 prefix, which Crossref registers for Defense Technical Information Center reports. **The same report can be reachable through Crossref and through the Defense Technical Information Center under two different labels**, and a rule keyed on where a record was found rather than on what it is would count such a report as secondary.

**The rule reaches the engine's documents and not the vehicle's.** The inlet, combustor and free-flight reports of the National Advisory Committee for Aeronautics are report primaries by this rule, and no record of the X-7 programme itself is among them. Journal papers by the engineers who did the work are not primaries by this rule either, so 17.3 percent is a lower bound on how much of this survey is first-hand.

### What the Survey Admits

**A record is admitted only when a person reading its title finds it on this article's subject**, which is the ramjet test vehicle, its inlet, its combustion, its heating, its boost and recovery, its instrumentation, the expendable method it stood for, and the engines and vehicles that inherited the problem.
