**The first correction was for permissiveness.** The bare term `booster` admitted a systematic

**The fourth correction came from a test rather than from a sample.** Rather than hunt further
instances, the gate acquired a check that proves the property, by asserting that no alternative in
one half of a conjunction can match inside another half. It failed immediately on a fifth instance

**The lesson is the one the gate module was written to teach, extended by one step.** A narrow gate
reports a small corpus and reads as a thin literature. A permissive gate reports a large corpus and
reads as thoroughness. **Only reading a random sample of both sides distinguishes those two.**
**Sampling does not, however, find a defect whose instances do not resemble each other**, and for
that the property has to be stated and tested rather than looked for.
