# X-Planes Drafting Notes

> **Navigation**: [Process](../README.md) | [Documentation Root](../../README.md)

The X-Planes articles do not report on their own drafting process. **Drafting notes live here, in process
files, and not in the article prose.** This directory holds, per article, every passage of drafting narrative
that was removed from the article on 9 October 2026, quoted in full with what replaced it, together with the
notes of the full readings of A319 and A325 made the same day.

What counts as drafting narrative is defined by the brief the removal followed, summarised here:

- named passes, harvest and pool histories, before and after counts, and dated repair paragraphs;
- corrections told as events, where the corrected claim stays in the article and the story moves here;
- the article's own verification code, scripts and checks;
- the series' internal writing rules, such as the genre note and the directive;
- openings that classify the article's own length or treatment, and internal cluster keys.

The article keeps the source facts, a present-tense methods statement, every recomputed survey number with its
rule, and an honest contamination statement. Earlier drafting history for each article is in the git history
and in the History table of [TASKLOG.md](../TASKLOG.md).
