### Corrections the primary record required

- National Aero-Space Plane, "cancellation in 1993", cited to a 1987 and a 1991 paper that predate it. Peebles 2007 gives approval in 1986 and cancellation in October 1994 after some 2.4 billion dollars. Corrected and cited. The X-30 article (A327) also says 1993 and was not edited.
- "The mishap investigation board convened five days after the first flight": the board report gives 5 June 2001, three days after. Corrected.
- Third flight Mach number 9.6: 9.6 is the separation target. The flight reports give a maximum powered Mach number of 9.68 and a separation at 9.736. Every flight-3 figure was recomputed at 9.68 (stagnation temperature 4,517 to 4,588 K, sonic static 3,764 to 3,824 K, total pressure ratio 32,345 to 34,178, speed 2,934 to 2,959 m/s, dynamic pressure 957 to 973 lbf/ft², specific thrust 147 to 148, Reynolds number 7.58 to 7.65 million). `tmp/a340/verify.py` now uses 9.68.
