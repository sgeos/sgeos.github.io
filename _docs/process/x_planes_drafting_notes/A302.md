### The Definition of Primary

**Primacy is a property of the document, and this survey derives it from the identifier.** A record is a report primary when it is a NASA Technical Reports Server record or carries a digital object identifier under the 10.21236 prefix, which Crossref registers for Defense Technical Information Center reports. By that rule 802 records are NASA Technical Reports Server records and 167 are Defense Technical Information Center reports. **The rule suits this aircraft unusually well**, because the X-5's flight reports, load measurements and model tests are research memoranda of the National Advisory Committee for Aeronautics and are held by the NASA Technical Reports Server.

**The rule still undercounts what is first-hand.** A journal or conference paper written by the people who flew or tested a configuration is a primary source for that configuration, and an identifier rule cannot see it, so the F-14 transition papers in the American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics series and the modern morphing flight tests are counted as secondary. 21.9 percent is a lower bound on how much of this survey is first-hand.

**Some records of doubtful relevance are kept.** Rotor blades with swept tips, crossflow instability on the rotating disk, ice accretion on swept wings and the wind-tunnel aerodynamics of tilt-wing models each share a phenomenon or a method with the swept-wing literature of this subject, so each is kept and a reader may weigh it accordingly.
