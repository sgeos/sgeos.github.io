### What the X-21 Actually Drew

The suction coefficient of $2 \times 10^{-4}$ used above is an assumption, and the record of the programme allows it to be tested. **The local suction coefficients the X-21 design called for were higher.** [Joslin 1998][ref_joslin_1998], summarising the Northrop final report, gives typical values of $v_{w}/V_{\infty}$ from $5 \times 10^{-4}$ in regions of nearly flat pressure to $10 \times 10^{-4}$ near the leading edge, and adds that at Mach 0.74 and 41,400 feet the measured suction distribution matched the predicted one in shape but exceeded it in level by 50 percent. Since the break-even coefficient above is fixed by the friction saving and the machinery, the margin at any other coefficient is simply
