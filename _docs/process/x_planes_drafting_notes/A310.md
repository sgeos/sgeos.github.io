> ### The Definition of Primary
>
> **Primacy is a property of the document, and this survey derives it from the identifier.** A record is a report primary when it is a NASA Technical Reports Server record or carries a digital object identifier under the 10.21236 prefix, which Crossref registers for Defense Technical Information Center reports. By that rule 809 primaries are Technical Reports Server records and 398 are Defense Technical Information Center reports. **The Langley reports that carry this article's numbers are report primaries under that rule**, the spin-tunnel series and the one-fifth-scale model tests among them.
>
> **The rule undercounts what is first-hand.** Conference papers of the American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics, abbreviated AIAA, and of the Vertical Flight Society, and journal papers by the engineers and pilots who built and flew aircraft of this class, are primary by any reading and are not reached by an identifier rule. The institutional records of the aircraft are cited as curated references and are not counted in the survey at all. **16.8 percent is therefore a lower bound on how much of the survey is first-hand.**
