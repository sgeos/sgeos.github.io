## A287 Governance — What Was Written

The article treats the sixth forcing-function condition, which requires that a venture absorb the capital its mission demands without transferring to the capital providers the authority to redirect the mission.

The formal spine is a control wedge separating voting rights from cash-flow rights. The cash-flow share follows a dilution recursion that declines monotonically in the round count, so under one-share-one-vote the founder control condition fails at a finite number of rounds determined by the initial share and the per-round dilution. The dual-class instrument decouples the voting recursion from the cash-flow recursion, which is the whole of its technical content. Everything else in the article concerns what the decoupling is used for.

### The analytical core

The OpenAI case supplies the article's central distinction. That structure had an unbounded formal wedge, since the controlling nonprofit board held all the votes and none of the equity. It was defeated in approximately five days. The reason is that formal control and effective control are different quantities, and effective control depends on a resource-dependence position the board did not hold. The article formalizes the condition under which a formally controlling body can actually prevail, in terms of the fraction of productive value embodied in mobile human capital and the fraction of personnel compensation contingent on equity value. A research organization sits far above the threshold, and no differently composed board would have fared better.

The SpaceX configuration has a modest formal wedge of roughly 1.9 and, unlike the OpenAI board, holds a resource-dependence position on the same side as the formal authority. That alignment, not the voting arithmetic, is what the article identifies as operative.

The Tesla comparison holds the individual constant and varies the governance configuration. The same person accepted a substantially weaker control position where capital was raised publicly, which indicates the configuration tracks the financing channel rather than the person.

## Analytical Content Added

The central analytical contribution of the completion pass is the distinction between a requirement stack assembled as a union of constituency requirements and one derived from a dominating mission requirement. The Space Shuttle case establishes that requirement breadth does not produce capability generality, because the Department of Defense crossrange requirement imposed a permanent configuration penalty in exchange for a capability the realized mission set never exercised. The three negation cases together establish three distinct failure modes that the abstract mechanic must separately exclude, and the historical precedent set establishes that the generality-forcing condition requires a cost condition that the capability condition does not imply. The Saturn V satisfies the capability condition and fails the cost condition.

The in-space-refueling requirement is identified as the strongest available discriminating evidence for the article's central thesis, because it admits no commercial derivation. The article states explicitly that the derivation claim is not directly verifiable from public sources and that the weight placed on the indirect evidence is proportionate to the weakness of the direct evidence.
