## The Claim the Article Is Built On

**Patience is not a temperament investors possess. It is a structural property instruments manufacture.**

The ordinary account attributes the outcome to investor conviction about the mission. That account is not false and it is not an explanation, because an investor's conviction does not relieve their fund of its obligation to return capital to limited partners on a schedule. The question is what arrangement let the conviction be acted upon.

### The constraint

A venture fund's life is roughly ten years, and it binds through three channels the commentary usually collapses into one:

- **contractual** — the partnership agreement obliges wind-up at term
- **compensatory** — carried interest is earned on *realization*, so an appreciated but unrealized position has paid the general partner nothing
- **reputational** — the successor fundraise is assessed on distributed capital, not carrying value, which puts the real clock on a three-to-four-year cycle

Against a position taken in 2008 and still held, the required holding period exceeds eighteen years — roughly twice the nominal fund life and five times the fundraise cycle. No quantity of conviction reconciles those figures.

### The resolution

The semi-annual tender mechanism, which the A287 Governance article treats from the control side. From the capital side it does something specific: it makes **realization independent of company exit**. A fund at term can realize at a market-tested price without the company undergoing any liquidity event. That triggers carry, produces the distribution, and fixes the distributed-capital metric — satisfying all three channels at once.

### The limitation I want to flag

The mechanism requires incoming buyers at a *rising* price, which means it works only where prospects are improving. **It supplies patience in good states and none in bad ones, which is the opposite of what the word ordinarily connotes.** A venture relying on it has not obtained capital that will wait through difficulty. It has obtained capital that need not leave during success. That is stated in the pattern extraction rather than buried.
