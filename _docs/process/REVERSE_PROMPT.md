## Questions for Human Review

- Verify the Birch Planet description is consistent with the intended concept. The article describes nested concentric shellworld layers around a supermassive black hole, powered by accretion. Paul Birch's 1991 paper originally described supramundane planets around Jovian bodies. The extrapolation to supermassive black holes was developed later by others. The eponymous name "Birch Planet" was popularized by Isaac Arthur.
- Verify that "originating corporation" is an acceptable replacement for "parent corporation." Other candidates considered: "founding corporation," "source corporation."
- Verify the six MathJax equations render correctly in local preview. Equations use inline `$...$` and display `$$...$$` syntax.
- Verify the EROI framing in the Mercury Cannibalization section. The article states EROI must exceed 1 for self-sustaining feedback, which is the precondition for the exponential growth described by Armstrong and Sandberg.
- The excess pledge share concentration equation assumes total corporate value $V_{total}$ is unchanged by the spinoff transaction. In practice, the market may reprice the originating corporation after the spinoff. The equation illustrates the mechanism, not a prediction.
