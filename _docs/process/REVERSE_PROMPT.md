New Blog Post: The Physics of Intergalactic Force Projection

Can a civilization in Andromeda actually project destructive force across 2.5 million light-years to the Milky Way? This article tests that assumption against known physics and finds that only one mechanism survives the analysis.

Key takeaways:
- Directed energy weapons and SMBH jets cannot maintain lethal energy density at intergalactic distances due to diffraction-limited beam divergence and inverse-square dilution.
- Self-replicating probe swarms are the only mechanism that combines intergalactic range with galaxy-scale destructive capability, using the target's own resources for exponential sterilization.
- Probe reliability over multimillion-year timescales is the weakest assumption and most important variable, with cosmic ray bit flip rates, evolutionary divergence, and parasitic replication failure posing fundamental engineering challenges.
