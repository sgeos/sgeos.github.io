## The Weight Rather Than the Shape Is the Majority of the Signature

**Integrating the lift term of the equivalent area over the whole aeroplane leaves the

design condition that is **4.232 square metres, a body 2.32 metres across**, against an
aeroplane whose fuselage is visibly slimmer.

**A designer may move that area along the length and may not remove it**, because removing
it means weighing less or flying faster and lower.

**AND THE TRADE WITH ALTITUDE RUNS THE WRONG WAY.** The lift term is inversely proportional
to dynamic pressure, so climbing to lengthen the propagation path enlarges the equivalent

forty thousand.** The two things altitude does to a sonic boom are opposed, and the article
labels the reading of that as its own inference.
