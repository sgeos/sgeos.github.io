# Article Errata

> **Navigation**: [Process](./README.md) | [Documentation Root](../README.md)

Known defects in published articles that are not yet fixed. Each entry is a claim that was checked against
the article on 2026-10-30 and either confirmed or found to need a source. Remove an entry when it is fixed or
refuted, and say so in the commit message.

## Origin

The agents that classified display equations for the Equations sections noted about 250 apparent errors.
Eight verification agents then checked each claim against its article under `tmp/findings/BRIEF.md`, which is
gitignored. **152 were fixed and 42 refuted.** The fixes were limited to what the article itself settles,
meaning arithmetic, units, signs, internal contradictions, typos and labels, and each fix that changed a value
also changed every value derived from it in the same article. A368's display subscripts, written as `\_`, were
fixed in the same pass. What remains is below.

- **Confirmed, not fixed.** The error is real, but the fix needs a judgement, such as a choice between two
  conventions, a cascade of many values, or a rewritten argument.
- **Needs a source.** Settling it needs the cited work or an outside fact. These were not decided from memory.
